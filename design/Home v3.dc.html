About

A legal practitioner and governance leader in intellectual property, regulated industries and complex commercial environments.

Liz Lenjo is an Advocate of the High Court of Kenya and the Founder and Managing Consultant of MyIP Legal Studio, a boutique advisory firm serving clients across Africa’s creative, digital, technology and energy sectors.

She chairs the Copyright Tribunal, teaches Media and Entertainment Law at Strathmore University Law School, and leads the Legal, Policy and Intellectual Property portfolio of the Kenya Fashion Council.
