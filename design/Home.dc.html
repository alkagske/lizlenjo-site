About Liz

Advocate of the High Court of Kenya

Liz Lenjo is an accomplished legal practitioner and governance leader with a distinguished record across intellectual property, regulated industries, and complex commercial environments. She is the Founder and Managing Consultant of MyIP Legal Studio, a boutique advisory firm serving clients across Africa’s creative, digital, technology, and energy sectors.

An adjunct faculty member at Strathmore University Law School, Liz teaches Media and Entertainment Law and also tutors with CopyrightX (HarvardX – Kenya).
